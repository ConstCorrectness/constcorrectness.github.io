Delete your ShelveShot account

ShelveShot · ConstCorrectness

You can delete your ShelveShot online account and its data at any time, either in the game or by email. You don't need to delete anything if you've only played offline: single-player progress stays on your device, and uninstalling the game removes it.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open ShelveShot and go to Versus › Online.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Online Account, then confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted right away.

By email

If you can't open the game, email horrible.cpp.guru@gmail.com with the subject "ShelveShot account deletion". Include your player name (shown under Profile in the game) and whether you sign in with Google Play Games or Game Center. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your online account and player ID, and its link to Google Play Games or Game Center
  • Your player name and friends list
  • Your ratings and leaderboard entries
  • Your coins, skins and claimed rewards
  • Challenges you sent and your challenge inbox

Coins and skins can't be restored after deletion, including ones you paid for.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase records (store, transaction ID, product, coins credited): up to 7 years, for refunds, fraud prevention and tax records.
  • Ranked match results: kept as part of your opponents' history, linked only to your deleted account's random ID, not your name.
  • Challenges other players sent you: kept for them, with your name replaced by "Deleted player".

Google and Apple keep their own records of your purchases and game sign-ins. To remove those, use your Google or Apple account settings.

For more detail, read the ShelveShot privacy policy and Terms of Service.
