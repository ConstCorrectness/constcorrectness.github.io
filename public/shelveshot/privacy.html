ShelveShot Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · ConstCorrectness

The short version: ShelveShot has no ads, doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. You can play the single-player game without an online account. If you play online, we keep a game account (an anonymous ID, the name you pick, your ratings, coins and friends) so online play works. Crash reports and usage statistics are sent only if you say yes when the game first asks, and you can change that in Settings. You can delete your online account inside the game at any time.

Who we are

ShelveShot is a game made by ConstCorrectness ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, email horrible.cpp.guru@gmail.com.

Services we use

We don't run our own servers. Online features run on these services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): sign-in, cloud saves, leaderboards, friends, matchmaking, multiplayer networking, game settings we can adjust remotely, and usage statistics (only with your consent). Unity's privacy policy
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): crash reports, only with your consent. Sentry's privacy policy
  • Google Play Games and Google Play Billing (Android): sign-in and purchases. Google's privacy policy
  • Apple Game Center and the App Store (iOS): sign-in and purchases. Apple's privacy policy

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your online data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your player ID, name, friends, ratings, leaderboard entries, wallet and the challenges you sent. A few records are kept because other players or the law depend on them:

  • Purchase records (store, transaction ID, product, coins credited) for up to 7 years, for refunds, fraud prevention and tax records.
  • Ranked match results stay as part of your opponents' history, linked only to your deleted account's random ID.
  • Challenges other players sent you stay with them, with your name replaced by "Deleted player".
  • Reports you made or that were made about you are kept for up to a year after review, so repeated abuse can be recognised.

Deleting your account also asks Unity Analytics to delete your usage statistics. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

See how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the game (Versus › Online › Profile › Delete Online Account), or by emailing us.
  • Change your name at any time in the game.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: turn them on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Report or block a player from their player card (tap their name). Blocked players' chat, friend requests and invites are hidden from you.
  • Play offline: the single-player game works without an online account.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have more rights, such as getting a copy of your data or objecting to how it's used. Email us to use them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

ShelveShot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Unity Gaming Services. Coins, ratings and purchases can only be changed by our server code, not by the game app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the game.

The rules for playing, coins and purchases are in our Terms of Service.
