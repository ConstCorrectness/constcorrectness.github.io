ShelveShot Support

ConstCorrectness

Need help? Email horrible.cpp.guru@gmail.com with your player name (it's on your profile in Versus › Online) and what happened. We answer within a few days.

Purchases

  • Coins didn't arrive? Open the store again while online: the game checks unfinished purchases with Google Play or the App Store and credits them. If they still don't appear, email us with the order ID from your receipt.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Google Play (request a refund) or the App Store (report a problem).
  • New phone? Sign in with Google Play Games or Game Center to bring your online account, coins and skins with you.

Players and chat

  • Report or block a player: tap their name (in a room, a match or a leaderboard) to open their player card, then press Report. Reporting also blocks them. Block only hides them without a report. Blocked players' chat, friend requests and invites are hidden from you.
  • Typed chat is only possible between friends and players sharing a room code. Matches with strangers use quick-chat phrases.
  • To report something urgent or a player who isn't in the game any more, email us.

Your account and data

  • Delete your online account (also possible in the game).
  • Turn crash reports and usage statistics on or off in Settings.
  • Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Something not working?

Tell us your device model, the app version (in your phone's app settings or on the store page) and what you were doing. If the game crashed and you allowed crash reports, we may already have the details.
